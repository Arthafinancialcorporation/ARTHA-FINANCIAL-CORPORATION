About us

About ARTHA FINANCIAL CORPORATION

ARTHA FINANCIAL CORPORATION is a sole proprietorship based in Kota, Rajasthan. We help businesses assess financing options, prepare loan applications and coordinate with lenders. Our proprietor has worked in financial services, loan advisory and loan sourcing since 2018.

We provide advisory and loan facilitation services. The relevant bank or NBFC determines eligibility, interest rates, security requirements, sanction and disbursement.

Consultation & Fees

Initial consultation is free. We discuss your funding needs and explain how we can assist.

ARTHA FINANCIAL CORPORATION does not charge customers a separate fee for standard loan referrals.

If you choose to engage us separately for an optional advisory assignment, such as reviewing financial projections or preparing a project finance proposal, we may quote a fixed fee or a fee payable in stages based on agreed milestones, where permitted by applicable rules and any relevant lender agreement.

Before work begins, we agree with you in writing on the scope, total fee, applicable taxes, payment schedule and any charges that apply if the assignment ends early.

Any bank or NBFC processing, valuation or legal charges are separate. Please refer to the lender’s documents for applicable charges.

Privacy Policy

The website enquiry form prepares an email message on your device. You can open it in your email app or copy it into a new email. ARTHA FINANCIAL CORPORATION receives your enquiry only after you send that email. The form itself does not send or store your entries on a website server.

Information you choose to share

ARTHA FINANCIAL CORPORATION receives the details you choose to send by email or provide when you call. An initial enquiry may include your name, company, city, phone number or email address, funding type, approximate amount and any optional details you provide.

How we use enquiry information

We use these details to respond to your enquiry, understand your funding needs, discuss possible advisory or referral services and follow up on work you request.

Sharing information with lenders

If you ask us to proceed with a lender referral or application, we will explain what information would be shared and for what purpose, and obtain your permission before sharing the necessary details with the relevant bank or NBFC, except where disclosure is required by law. If supporting documents are needed later, we will explain what to provide and how to send them.

Retention and privacy enquiries

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond, follow up or provide services you request, and longer where required by law.
